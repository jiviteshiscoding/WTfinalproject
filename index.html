Welcome to Survey Commission Portal

This portal is developed to collect public demographic information, education details, and citizen feedback for nation-building and policy development initiatives.

Citizens can participate by filling out the online survey registration form or submitting direct suggestions through our feedback section.

Portal Highlights & Objectives

The Survey Commission website provides a simple, direct interface for citizens and administrators:

  • Online Registration: Fill out personal details, educational qualifications, and technical interests in a structured form.
  • Citizen Feedback: Share valuable opinions, grievances, and suggestions for public service improvements.
  • Officer Gateway: Secure login for Administrators and Survey Officers to review records.
  • Simple & Accessible: Designed with standard HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for fast, lightweight local browsing.

About Us

The Survey Commission portal is a student web development project designed to simulate online data collection and citizen feedback forms using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
